Method & sources

The planner combines open data sources into a transparent, factor-by-factor assessment. It does not produce a probability of incident: every indicator is shown separately so the analyst can weigh it.

  • Routing and alternatives: OSRM, on OpenStreetMap data.
  • Geocoding: Nominatim (OpenStreetMap).
  • Points of interest within the route corridor (hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, police, fire stations, embassies and consulates, fuel, hotels, transit stations, bridges and tunnels): Overpass API (OpenStreetMap).
  • Weather and daylight at origin, midpoint and destination for the departure hour: Open-Meteo.
  • Crime exposure (England, Wales and Northern Ireland only): data.police.uk, latest available month. Street-level crimes within 150 m of each route segment, weighted by category, expressed per km and ranked relative to the other routes analysed. This is a relative index, not a probability.
  • Analyst risk zones: polygons drawn and rated by the user, intended for areas where no open crime data exists or where local knowledge prevails.
  • Medical access: straight-line distance from each point of the route to the nearest mapped hospital.

Open data can be incomplete or out of date. The tool supports, and does not replace, a professional route assessment and reconnaissance.
